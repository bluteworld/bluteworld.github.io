BLUTES — IMAGE DESCRIPTIONS
==========================

Every image in this folder is a "blute": a small, cloud-shaped creature drawn as a
scalloped blob of rounded lobes, outlined in a thick, hand-drawn dark brown stroke.
Blutes have no separate head or body — the whole silhouette is the face. Features are
minimal: two eyes (dots, ovals, or closed curved lashes), a pair of arched brows,
optional cheek dimples, a simple curved mouth, plus thin noodle arms that end in small
round knobs and two thin legs that end in little rounded feet. Every drawing sits on a
transparent background and uses the same restrained palette — muted sage and olive
greens, dusty blue-grey, mustard yellow, terracotta, rosy mauve, soft lavender, and
warm tan — all outlined in the same dark brown. Blute body color varies image to image
and does not indicate a different character. A few images are signed "@blute_world" or
"@bloot_world" in faint pencil-style script.

HOW BLUTE EXPRESSIONS WORK
--------------------------

Because a blute has no body language above the shoulders — the silhouette *is* the face —
almost all of the emotional range in these 71 images is carried by four things: the eyes,
the brows, the mouth, and a small set of external symbols floating nearby. The vocabulary
is consistent enough across the folder to treat as a system.

EYES. Six recurring forms, in rough order of frequency:
  • Two solid dark dots or ovals — eyes open, attentive, emotionally neutral. The default
    for "doing a task": doctor, chef, farmer, mining, groceries, picnic, pickaxe, hiker.
  • Closed lashes arcing UPWARD (∪) — active happiness, laughing, celebrating:
    glad, dancing, jumprope, icecream, swing, envelope-adjacent, photobooth, waving.
  • Closed lashes arcing DOWNWARD (∩) — calm, blissful, absorbed, savouring:
    bathtub, ramen, pizza, singer, headphones, shower, pottery, daydreaming, cozy.
  • Drooping / sagging lashes with the brows sloping down — sadness or sleepiness:
    sad, sobbing, yawning, sighing, moving.
  • Narrow flat slits — focus and determination: gamer, chef_pan, photographer, surfer,
    yoga, tennis.
  • Hard angled slits, scrunched — strain or physical reaction: lemon, runner.
Two special cases sit outside the system: ghost.PNG uses hollow ringed eyes with floating
pupils (the only genuinely eerie face in the set), and several blutes have their eyes
concealed by a prop — binoculars in birdwatching, a sleep mask in eye_mask, tinted lenses
in glasses, a camera body in photographer, a monocle over one eye in monocle.

BROWS. Thin high arches read as pleasant or curious and pair with most neutral faces.
Brows angled down and inward into a V signal determination or displeasure (gamer, tennis,
surfer, reluctant_agreement, suitcase_late, lemon, runner). Brows sloping down toward the
outside of the face signal sadness (sad, sobbing). Asymmetric brows — one flat, one raised
— signal doubt, skepticism, or appraisal (confused, sandcastles, monocle, blushing).

MOUTHS. The single most expressive element, and the widest vocabulary:
  • Small symmetrical upward arc — plain contentment (glad).
  • Very wide sweeping smile, nearly the full width of the body — deep comfort or bliss
    (armchair, yoga, daydreaming, jumprope, pottery).
  • Open solid half-moon — shouting, cheering, laughing out loud (excited, envelope, swing,
    photobooth, waving, umbrella, flying_kite).
  • Small round "o" — singing, humming, quiet awe (shower, headphones, singer,
    birdwatching, surfer).
  • Flat dash or straight line — withholding, guarded, or purely focused (curtain, tennis,
    gamer, photographer, sandcastles).
  • Wavering S-curve — confusion or discomfort (confused, i_dont_know,
    reluctant_agreement).
  • Downward arc — sadness (sad); small open wail with tears — crying (sobbing).
  • Jagged open rectangle — panic or gritted effort (suitcase_late, runner).
  • Twisted grimace — sourness (lemon).
  • Puckered loop — a kiss (blowing_a_kiss). Tall open oval — a yawn (yawning).
  • A small dark knob or dot resting at one end of the smile is a recurring shorthand for
    a hand raised to the blute's own face: bashfulness, dreaminess, or "where do I start"
    (blushing, in_love, winter_blushing, dancing, moving, yoga, sighing).

CHEEKS. Soft orange or red blush patches appear on roughly a dozen blutes and always add
warmth rather than embarrassment on their own — affection, shyness, cosiness, or simple
good cheer (blowing_a_kiss, blushing, in_love, icecream, work, doctor, hot_air_balloon,
winter_blushing, bathtub, bubbles).

FLOATING SYMBOLS. A small set of external marks do work the face can't:
  • Hearts — red or terracotta hearts mean active, directed affection (blowing_a_kiss,
    in_love); dark brown hearts drawn in the outline colour mean vague, unfocused
    infatuation (daydreaming).
  • Musical notes — singing aloud (singer). Lettered "Z"s — sleepiness (yawning).
  • Curved motion arcs — speed or repetition (dancing, jumprope, runner, tennis,
    dog_fetch, surfer's spray).
  • A small scalloped puff-cloud at the mouth — an audible exhale (sighing).

ONE THING THAT IS NOT AN EXPRESSION: body colour. The same emotion appears in sage green,
blue-grey, mauve, mustard, and terracotta across different images, and the same colour
carries opposite moods (sage green is both yawning and jumprope). Colour varies for visual
variety, not to encode feeling or to distinguish separate characters.


OTHER ATTRIBUTES THAT VARY ACROSS THE SET
-----------------------------------------

The face carries the emotion, but several other axes vary image to image and are worth
cataloguing on their own.

ARMS AND HANDS. The noodle arms are the second most expressive element after the mouth,
and they have their own small vocabulary:
  • Both arms flung up and out in a V — celebration (excited, hot_air_balloon).
  • One arm raised, one relaxed — greeting or presenting (waving, envelope, icecream,
    pickaxe, farmer, mining, hiker, blowing_a_kiss).
  • Both arms raised and bent, knob hands turned palm-up — the shrug (i_dont_know).
  • Arms crossed over the body — refusal, defensiveness (reluctant_agreement).
  • One arm looped up and over the top of the head — scratching in confusion (confused),
    or slack and deflated (sighing, yawning).
  • Both arms brought forward to cradle or hug an object — armchair, groceries, ramen,
    pottery, photographer, sandcastles.
  • Arms tucked away entirely, nothing breaking the silhouette — stillness and interiority
    (daydreaming, yoga, cozy, sad).
Hands are never drawn with fingers; every arm terminates in the same round knob, so grip
is always implied by contact alone.

LEGS AND STANCE. Four recurring postures: planted and even (the neutral default — glad,
waving, sad); mid-stride with one leg forward (any blute going somewhere — hiker,
groceries, work, suitcase_late); both feet clearly off the ground mid-jump (excited, swing,
runner, jumprope, dancing); and braced wide for effort (chef_pan, pickaxe, surfer, tennis).
yoga is the sole outlier, balancing on one leg with the other folded up. In roughly a dozen
images the legs are hidden altogether — under bathwater (bathtub), behind a wheel housing
(pottery), inside a quilt (cozy), below a basket rim (hot_air_balloon), or behind stacked
props (moving, groceries, sobbing).

BODY TILT. Several blutes are drawn rotated off-vertical rather than upright, and the tilt
consistently signals a destabilised state — sleepiness in yawning, swooning in in_love,
shyness in winter_blushing, momentum in dancing and excited, bashfulness in blushing. An
upright blute is a composed blute.

HEADWEAR. Ten images put something on the blute's head: pleated chef's toque (chef,
chef_pan), top hat (fancy, monocle), straw sun hat (farmer), bush hat with chin cord
(hiker), hard hat (mining), striped party cone (party), knitted pom-pom beanie
(winter_blushing), sweatband (runner), flower crown (skirt), and a small bow (picnic).
Headwear is frequently drawn nearly as large as the blute wearing it.

EYEWEAR AND FACE GEAR. Rectangular reading glasses (armchair), round tinted glasses
(glasses), monocle on a chain (monocle), binoculars (birdwatching), a lash-painted sleep
mask (eye_mask), a camera pressed to one eye (photographer), and an over-ear headset with
boom mic (headphones). In every case the gear covers or replaces at least one eye, which is
why these blutes read differently from the rest of the set.

FACIAL HAIR. A large dark curled handlebar moustache recurs on exactly five blutes — chef,
chef_pan, fancy, monocle, and photobooth, where it is a stick-held prop rather than real
hair. It reads as status or performance every time it appears.

CLOTHING. Most blutes are bare, so the seven that wear real garments stand out sharply:
doctor's white coat (doctor), dungarees (farmer), top and skirt with a crossbody bag
(skirt), tailcoat and waistcoat with bow tie (fancy), a wrapped quilt (cozy), scarf and
boots (winter_blushing), and a climbing harness (rock_climber). runner adds a pinned race
bib without any other clothing.

PROPS BY DOMAIN. Food and drink (icecream, pizza, ramen, lemon, groceries, work's coffee,
armchair's teacup); tools and work (pickaxe, mining's spade, brick_laying's trowel,
farmer's pitchfork, artist's easel and palette, pottery's wheel, programmer's mouse and
screen, doctor's stethoscope and prescription, photographer's camera); play and sport
(jumprope, tennis, surfer, swing, flying_kite, sandcastles, bubbles, rock_climber);
performance (singer's mic, party's blower, photobooth's props, headphones); travel and
transit (suitcase_late, hot_air_balloon, umbrella, hiker's pole, moving's boxes); and
domestic life (bathtub, shower, cozy, envelope, picnic's basket and flower).

SCALE PLAY. A recurring comic device: the prop is deliberately drawn far too big for the
blute holding it. The pizza slice spans half the image width; the tennis racquet and the
chef's toque are each about as tall as the blute; the party hat matches the blute's own
height; the skipping rope arcs to roughly twice it; the grocery bag nearly swallows the
carrier. In the most extreme cases — sitting_under_flower, hot_air_balloon, surfer,
armchair — the blute is a small figure inside a much larger object.

VIEWPOINT AND CROP. Most blutes face the viewer straight on. A handful are three-quarter
(blushing, chef_pan, confused, sandcastles) or in profile (brick_laying, bathtub, surfer,
and both trotting dogs). Four are drawn entirely from behind with no face at all — artist,
brick_laying, rock_climber, programmer — which makes them the only images in the folder
with no expression to read. Separately, about a third of the set crops or occludes the
figure: half-hidden behind a curtain (curtain), cut off at the waterline (bathtub), behind
a wheel (pottery), inside a quilt (cozy), over a basket rim (hot_air_balloon), or behind
piled props (moving, groceries, sobbing, sandcastles).

CAST AND STAGING. Blutes are always alone. No image in this folder contains two blutes, and
no blute ever appears alongside the dog. The dog gets three solo images of its own
(dog_fetch, golden_dog, tiny_dog), and the only cross-species interaction anywhere is the
bird perched on the binoculars in birdwatching. Likewise, most images have no environment
at all — the blute floats on transparency with no ground line, no horizon, and no
background fill. Only a minority are genuinely staged: armchair, bathtub, rock_climber,
sandcastles, moving, hot_air_balloon, surfer, sitting_under_flower, pottery, swing, and
brick_laying.

TEXT AND SIGNATURES. In-image text is nearly always deliberately illegible scribble — the
race bib number (runner), the prescription (doctor), the code or notes on the screen
(programmer). The single exception is the book in armchair, hand-lettered "THE BLOOT IN THE
HAT." Three images carry a faint pencil-script watermark: cozy and rock_climber are signed
"@blute_world", while flying_kite is signed "@bloot_world" — note the different spelling.

SHADING. Fills are flat almost everywhere. The few images that model light do it with a
single simple device: a translucent wedge of lamp-glow (armchair), a diagonal shadow band
where the canopy edge falls across the face (umbrella), a soft shadow pool on the ground
(bathtub), panel seams on a curved surface (hot_air_balloon), and fabric fold shading
(cozy, fancy).

armchair.png
A mustard-yellow blute sits sunk deep into an oversized olive-green wing-back armchair,
reading. It wears rectangular wire-frame glasses, its brows are relaxed, and its mouth
is a wide contented smile that stretches almost the full width of its face. Both noodle
arms come forward to hold open a small dusty-pink hardback book, whose right-hand page
carries hand-lettered text reading "THE BLOOT IN THE HAT." Behind and above, an arched
floor lamp with a slate-blue shade leans over the chair, and a pale translucent wedge of
lampglow falls diagonally across the whole scene. To the left, a tiny round side table
holds a blue-grey teacup on a saucer with three curls of steam rising. The overall read
is a cozy evening in — one of the most fully staged scenes in the set.

artist.png
A dusty blue-grey blute is drawn from behind, so no face is visible at all — just the
scalloped silhouette of its back and two thin legs. It stands at a tall wooden A-frame
easel on the left, one arm reaching up and around to touch a paintbrush to the canvas.
The cream-colored canvas already shows a painting in progress: a scallop-outlined blute
shape with a blue-grey blob inside it, plus a loose red dot and a green dot floating
nearby — the blute is painting a portrait of a blute. In its other hand, on the right
side, it holds a cream oval palette dabbed with blue, orange-red, and olive-green paint.
The composition is almost entirely line-work and negative space.

bathtub.png
A terracotta-orange blute is submerged up to its cheeks in a slate-blue, claw-footed
freestanding bathtub, seen from the side. Only the top few lobes of its silhouette rise
above the waterline. Its eyes are closed into gentle downward lashes, its mouth is a
small satisfied curve, and both cheeks carry little curved dimple marks. Heaped mounds
of pale lilac-white bubble foam pile along the rim of the tub and over the blute's head,
with a few loose bubble clusters drifting free in the air on either side. One foam clump
to the right is shaped like a heart. A soft olive shadow pools under the tub's four
clawed feet. No arms or legs are visible — they're under the water.

birdwatching.png
A blue-grey blute faces forward wearing an enormous pair of round binoculars whose two
grey-rimmed lenses cover most of its face, the strap looping around behind. Its brows
arch high above the eyepieces and its mouth is a small vertical "o" of surprise. The
joke is in the framing: a mustard-yellow songbird has landed directly on the right
barrel of the binoculars, wings spread, tail out, tiny black eye and long dark beak
pointing left — so the bird it's hunting for is sitting on the instrument, inches from
its face and completely unnoticed. Two thin legs dangle below; no arms are raised.

blowing_a_kiss.PNG
A warm tan-yellow blute stands facing the viewer, blowing a kiss. Its eyes are closed
into two downturned lashes under high arched brows, its cheeks carry soft rosy blush
patches, and its mouth is puckered into a small looped kiss shape. Its right arm (viewer's
right) reaches up and out to the side, having just released the kiss; a small solid
terracotta-red heart floats in the air just past the knob of that hand. The left arm
hangs down loosely. Both legs are planted in a light stride. Nothing else is in frame —
the heart is the only prop.

blushing.PNG
A dusty rose-pink blute stands three-quarters on, caught mid-bashful. Its eyes are two
solid dark ovals set slightly off-center, its brows are asymmetrically arched, and its
mouth is a long lopsided smile with a small dark dot pressed into the left end of it,
as though it were biting back the expression. Two soft red-blush glows bloom on the
cheeks. One arm curls up shyly toward its own face on the right side while the other
tucks in low. The pose is a small, self-conscious shuffle — legs slightly apart, feet
turned outward. No props at all; the whole image is carried by the blush and the crooked
mouth.

brick_laying.PNG
A salmon-terracotta blute stands in profile facing right, working on a half-built brick
wall that fills the right third of the frame. The wall is drawn as loose staggered rows
of individual bricks in two alternating browns, set in grey-tan mortar, with the topmost
courses uneven as if still going up. The blute has no visible face in this image — it's
turned fully away from the viewer — and its single visible noodle arm reaches up to press
a fresh dollop of pale grey mortar onto a brick with a small trowel-shaped blob. Two thin
legs stand slightly apart at the bottom left. It reads as quiet, absorbed manual labor.

bubbles.PNG
A dusty rose-mauve blute blows soap bubbles. Its eyes are closed into two curved lashes,
its brows arch calmly, and its mouth is pursed around the end of a small grey bubble
wand, drawn as a slim handle with a looped ring at the tip. A thin dark line runs from
the wand down across the body to where a hand holds a small bubble-solution loop. Three
translucent pale-grey bubbles float free at the right of the frame — one still attached
at the wand's ring, two drifting off. Faint blush patches sit on both cheeks. The mood
is idle, absorbed play.

chef.png
A sage-green blute in a very tall, pleated cream chef's toque stands facing forward. The
hat is nearly as large as the blute itself, with five puffed fluted panels above a broad
banded brim. Its eyes are two solid dark dots under thin arched brows, and beneath them
sits a large curled handlebar moustache, drawn in the same dark brown as the outlines,
with a tiny smile visible just under it. Its left arm (viewer's left) hangs loosely; its
right arm is raised, holding up a dark wooden spoon. Two thin legs stride below. This is
the classic "proud chef" portrait of the set.

chef_pan.png
The same moustachioed sage-green chef blute, this time in three-quarter profile facing
right and mid-cook. The pleated cream toque sits atop its head. Its eyes are narrowed to
two flat confident slits, the curled handlebar moustache dominates the lower face, and
its mouth curves in a broad self-satisfied grin. One long arm extends far to the right,
holding the handle of a dark brown frying pan tipped up in a flip — the pan's contents
are drawn as a couple of tumbling dark blobs airborne above it, with motion arcs curving
on either side to show the toss. Both legs are set in a wide, braced stance.

confused.PNG
A blue-grey blute is caught mid-puzzlement. One brow is drawn flat and low while the
other arches sharply up, its two eyes are small dark ovals, and its mouth is a single
wavering S-curve line — neither smile nor frown. Its left arm reaches up and back over
the top of its own head to scratch at it, the elbow forming a wide loop above the
silhouette; the right arm hangs out to the side. The legs are mismatched, one straight
and one bent as though it stopped walking halfway through the thought. There are no
props — the confusion is entirely in the eyebrows and the wobbling mouth.

cozy.PNG
A mustard-yellow blute is bundled into an enormous sage-green padded quilt or puffer
duvet, drawn as thick overlapping folded panels that fill the bottom two-thirds of the
frame. Only a wedge of the blute's face peeks out from the hood of the wrapping — two
eyes closed into contented downward lashes, and a small upward-curving smile. No arms or
legs are visible; the blute is completely cocooned. The image is signed "@blute_world" in
faint script along the lower edge of the quilt. It reads as pure warmth and comfort.

curtain.PNG
A mustard-yellow blute peeks out from behind a heavy dusty-mauve stage or window curtain
that occupies the entire left half of the frame, drawn with long vertical drape folds and
a gathered, swagged edge. Only the right half of the blute is visible — one arched brow,
one dark oval eye, and a flat straight dash of a mouth showing no expression. One noodle
arm hangs down on the exposed right side, and one leg steps out from under the curtain's
hem at the bottom. The effect is hesitant: something between hiding, eavesdropping, and
working up the nerve to come out.

dancing.PNG
A sage-green blute is mid-dance, tilted at a jaunty angle. Its eyes are closed into two
happy curved lashes, and its mouth is a long sweeping smile with a solid dark dot at its
center. One arm is thrown up high and out to the upper left, knob-hand raised; the other
sweeps low across the body to the right. The legs are crossed and kicking, one foot
pointing away from the other, so the whole figure looks unbalanced in a deliberate,
joyful way. Two curved motion arcs sit in the air to the lower right, indicating a
swing or a spin. No props.

daydreaming.png
An olive-green blute stands facing forward with its eyes closed into two soft downward
lashes and its mouth in a wide, dreamy, entirely relaxed smile. Both arms are tucked
loosely behind or beside its body, so almost nothing juts out from the silhouette. Above
its head, two solid dark brown hearts float upward at different sizes and angles — the
only props in the frame. Because the hearts are drawn in the same dark brown as the
outlines rather than in red, the mood reads as vague, drifting infatuation rather than
active romance.

doctor.png
A mustard-yellow blute wears a full white doctor's coat with a notched collar, four
buttons down the front, and short sleeves that the thin noodle arms emerge from. A dark
stethoscope hangs around its neck, the two tube-ends draping down over the lapels. Its
eyes are two solid dark dots, its brows are thin arches, both cheeks carry soft orange
blush patches, and its mouth is a small, reassuring smile. Its right hand (viewer's
right) holds up a small cream slip of paper covered in illegible scribbled squiggles —
a prescription. Two short legs poke out beneath the coat. It's a warm, friendly bedside
manner in one image.

dog_fetch.PNG
This image has no blute in it at all. Instead it shows the blute-world dog: a golden-tan
cartoon dog with darker tan floppy ears, a darker tan patch across its crown, and a
darker tan tail, drawn in the same thick dark-brown outline style. The dog is captured
mid-leap on its hind legs, front paws lifted, mouth wide open with a small pink tongue
lolling out, one tiny curved closed eye and a dark round nose. A yellow-green tennis
ball with a curved seam pattern floats in the air above and to the right of its head,
with two motion arcs below and behind it showing the ball's flight. Pure fetch-time
enthusiasm.

envelope.PNG
A mustard-yellow blute cheers with a piece of mail. Its eyes are two solid dark dots
under high arched brows, and its mouth is wide open in a big grinning half-moon — one of
the broadest smiles in the whole set. Its left arm is thrown up and out to the side, knob
hand raised in celebration; its right arm is raised high above its head, holding aloft a
sealed envelope drawn as a small dark rectangle with a pale blue-grey flap panel. Both
legs kick out in a stride, one clearly off the ground. The read is unambiguous good news
just arrived.

excited.PNG
A soft lavender-purple blute — one of the few purple ones — is caught mid-celebration.
Its eyes are squeezed shut into two upward curved lashes under high arched brows, and its
mouth is thrown wide open into a large solid half-circle of a shout. Both arms are flung
up and outward in a V, each ending in a raised round knob hand. Both legs are kicked out
mid-jump, feet clearly off the ground, and the whole body is tilted slightly to one side
from the momentum. There are no props or effects — the pose alone does all the work.

eye_mask.PNG
A blue-grey blute wears a padded sleep mask pushed over its eyes, drawn as two joined
almond-shaped cups with long comically painted-on eyelashes fanning up from the top edge
of each one, and a thin elastic strap looping around behind its head. Under the mask its
real eyes are closed into small contented curves, and its mouth is a wide serene smile.
One arm hangs down at the left, the other reaches out to the right. Two thin legs stand
loosely apart. The joke is the fake glamour lashes drawn on the mask itself — sleeping
in style.

fancy.png
A mustard-yellow blute is dressed for a formal evening in full black tie. It wears a
tall black top hat with a wide curled brim, a black tailcoat with dark lapels over a
cream waistcoat with four small buttons, and a black bow tie. A dark curled handlebar
moustache sits under two dark dot eyes and sharply angled confident brows. Its right
hand (viewer's right) holds up a slim black walking cane with a curved handle; its left
sleeve ends in a small dark cuff. Two little dark shoes poke out below the coat. Unlike
most blutes, the costume here covers the whole silhouette, so it reads almost like a
tiny gentleman rather than a blob.

farmer.png
A blue-grey blute stands in a wide-brimmed straw sun hat in mustard yellow with a
darker gold underbrim, and brown canvas dungarees with shoulder straps, a bib, and
stitched knee creases that cover the lower two-thirds of its body. Its eyes are two dark
dots under thin arched brows, and its mouth is a small friendly smile. Its left arm hangs
down; its right arm reaches up to grip the long handle of a three-tined pitchfork that
stands taller than the blute itself, planted at the right edge of the frame. Two short
legs step out below the dungarees.

flying_kite.png
An olive-green blute flies a kite on a breezy day. Its eyes are closed into two curved
happy lashes, and its mouth is open in a small delighted grin. Its left arm reaches up
and across to hold a thin dark string that runs diagonally up to the top-left corner,
where a pale blue-grey diamond kite tilts in the wind, its cross-spars drawn in and a
long wavy tail streaming off the bottom. The right arm swings out to the side for
balance, and both legs are spread in a wide stride as though walking backward to keep
the kite aloft. The kite face is signed "@bloot_world" in faint pencil script.

gamer.png
A mustard-yellow blute is locked in intense concentration on a game. Its brows are drawn
down into a hard determined V, its eyes are two narrow flat slits, and its mouth is a
small tight dash — the most focused, least friendly expression in the collection. Held
up in front of its lower body is a large solid dark-brown gaming controller, gripped in
both knob hands, with a long cable snaking loosely away off the bottom-right of the
frame. Two thin legs are planted below and behind the controller. The comedy is entirely
in the mismatch between the cute blob and the utterly serious game face.

ghost.PNG
A blute in ghost form, and one of the few images that abandons the standard face. The
body is drawn in pale bone-white with the usual dark brown outline, but the bottom edge
is drawn as three trailing, tapered wisps instead of legs and feet, so it hovers rather
than stands. Its two eyes are hollow mustard-yellow almond rings with solid dark pupils
floating inside, set at slightly different heights and sizes — unsettling rather than
cute. There is no mouth at all. Three short curved crease lines mark the folds of the
sheet-like body. No arms extend from the silhouette.

glad.PNG
A warm tan-yellow blute in the simplest, most default happy pose in the set. Its eyes
are closed into two gently curved lashes beneath softly arched brows, and its mouth is a
clean symmetrical upward smile centered on the face. Both noodle arms hang out to the
sides, relaxed, ending in round knob hands; both legs are planted evenly with feet turned
slightly out. There are no props, effects, clothes, or scenery of any kind — just an
uncomplicated, quietly contented blute standing on nothing.

glasses.png
A dusty rose-mauve blute wears a pair of large round spectacles with thick dark rims and
tinted tan-brown lenses, the arms of the frames reaching back past the edges of its face.
Behind the lenses, its two pupils are drawn in dark olive-green rather than the usual
brown, giving the eyes an unusual depth. Its brows arch high above the frames and its
mouth is a small modest smile. Both arms extend out to the sides and slightly down, and
two thin legs stand loosely apart. The glasses are the entire subject — a portrait, not
an activity.

golden_dog.png
Another dog-only image with no blute present: the golden retriever of blute-world, drawn
sitting upright and facing the viewer. Its coat is warm mustard-tan with darker tan floppy
ears, a darker tan tail curling out to the left, and darker tan paw-tips on the four feet
at the bottom. Its face is minimal in the blute style — two closed curved smiling eyes,
no visible mouth, and a small dark rounded triangle for a nose. The rounded chest and
haunches echo the scalloped blute silhouette. Calm, seated, and content.

groceries.PNG
A blue-grey blute walks home carrying shopping. Its eyes are two solid dark dots under
thin arched brows, and its mouth is a long, mildly weary curve. Both noodle arms come
across the front of its body to hug a large brown paper grocery bag, drawn with a folded
top rim, side panel, and a couple of crease lines, that covers most of its lower half.
Poking out of the top of the bag are two red tomatoes and a spray of dark olive leafy
